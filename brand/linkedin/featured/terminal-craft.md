**Terminal-Craft — tmux and nvim fluency for AI-coding work**

A ten-lesson curriculum I built and drilled on myself, because the thing slowing me down in
AI-assisted work wasn't the model — it was reading long diffs and juggling several agents with
arrow keys. tmux for running agents side by side, nvim for fast review and editing, plus shell
and dotfiles. No plugin manager and no IDE rebuild: nine of the ten lessons ran on an untouched
baseline config. Every lesson starts from a real scenario and ends with a live drill, and the
site logs what went wrong as well as what worked, including the lesson that taught macros before
it taught undo.
